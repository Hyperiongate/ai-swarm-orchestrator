Why Survey
Because Assumptions Cost Money
Most operations leaders think they know what their workforce wants. The data tells a different story — every time.
"We assumed everyone hated 12-hour shifts. The survey showed 68% preferred them." — Plant Manager, Pharmaceutical Manufacturing
📈

Benchmark Against Your Industry

Compare every answer to normative data from hundreds of similar operations. Know instantly whether your overtime fairness score, schedule satisfaction, or safety perception is above or below average.

🎯

Identify What Actually Drives Turnover

It's rarely pay alone. Our survey isolates the specific schedule features, management practices, and working conditions that push your best people out the door — or keep them.

🤝

Give Employees a Voice Before Change

The most successful schedule changes involve employees early. A survey builds buy-in before the first shift rotates. Employees who feel heard resist change less.

📊

Data-Driven Decisions, Not Politics

When 73% of your workforce says overtime distribution is unfair, that's not a feeling — it's a fact. Survey data replaces opinion with evidence and makes every stakeholder conversation easier.

What You Receive
Professional Analysis, Not Just a Spreadsheet
Every survey produces a comprehensive deliverable package — benchmarked, analyzed, and ready for leadership presentation.

Normative Benchmarking

Every question compared against data from hundreds of operations. Your scores in green (above norm), red (below norm), or gray (at norm). Instantly see where you lead and where you lag.

Breakout by Department, Shift & Tenure

Results sliced by every demographic group. See how 3rd shift feels about overtime versus 1st shift. Compare veteran attitudes to new hires. Find the pockets that need attention.

Expert Recommendations

Not just numbers — a written analysis of your top strengths, primary concerns, and specific action items. Built from 30+ years of knowing what the data means in operations like yours.
